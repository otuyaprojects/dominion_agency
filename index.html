FAQ

Questions, answered.

A few things clients usually want to know before starting a project.

What kind of software does Dominion build? +

We build custom business systems, web applications, dashboards, SaaS products, management platforms and other digital products tailored to specific business requirements.

Can Dominion work with an existing system? +

Yes. We can work with existing applications, modernize older systems, integrate APIs and gradually improve architecture without rebuilding everything unnecessarily.

How does a project begin? +

We begin with a discovery conversation where we understand the business problem, desired outcome, scope and technical requirements.

Do you provide support after launch? +

Yes. Depending on the engagement, we can provide maintenance, improvements, monitoring, technical support and continued product development.
